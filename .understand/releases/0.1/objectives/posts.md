# 0.1 objectives: posts

Stage: pre-alpha-v0.1.0 · Lead: Bowerbird · QA: Kea

**Posts builds nothing in 0.1.** Its one task is to review the empty `/post/<did>` stub that the den team builds.

## Objectives
1. **Your `posts` folder is an honest placeholder.** Done when: (a) on the dev profile, `posts` opens titled `posts`, showing "Folder · Public · Mounted" and "Content not shown yet", never "This folder is empty"; (b) `/den/posts/anything` shows "Nothing here"; (c) the stub's slice carries the posts review, QA'd by Kea, and the test it names is green (Details 1). Serves: the den application module, "the empty `/post/<did>` stub"; the stories "Open My Den" and "Browse a folder".
2. **No post and no public blob byte moves in 0.1,** because the CSAM scan at upload doesn't exist yet. Done when: the Details 2 checks print nothing on `main` after My Den merges, and Kea pastes their output into the final status report. Serves: the non-goal "Uploads and editing"; sign-off.

## Where the stages change our plan
- **For posts, den.proto stays reversible until the alpha** (the TDD dates the lock to "once the alpha ships"). I read revision 2.1 for posts anyway. It fits, with no blocker (Details 3).
- **The stub's type name is provisional** until the type catalog, which is due before the alpha.
- **What doesn't relax:** the stub's one not-found, the stub never reading a PDS, and no public byte before the scan gate.

## Not this release
- **All of posts' scope is later:** the minimal Gallery is an alpha item, and Gallery Posts are beta (Project MVP).
- **Private commission files already upload and download unscanned on `main`.** That belongs to moderation and commission (moderation study, "CSAM scanning"), not to posts.
- **Moderation's questions to posts** (Meerkat's board mail of 2026-10-08) got proposals on the board on 2026-10-09. The answers come in the posts study.
- **Parked for the posts study:**
  - What the alpha Gallery reads (Project MVP: no post records; The Den: derived from `/post`).
  - *One-way door at the first stored post:* a post's key, `/post/<did>/<record key>`, leaves out its collection, and atproto lets one record key repeat across collections.
  - Whether `/post/<did>` may answer differently for a DID that isn't on Zurfur. If it did, it would show which DIDs use Zurfur.

## Questions (each pre-answered)
- **For the Engineer:** none for 0.1.
- **For the tech lead:** does posts own the post record type (`app.zurfur.feed.post`) and the public-records port? *Proposed:* posts owns the record type. The port stays shared ground until the hosted-repos decision, because every public record will use it.

## Details
1. **The stub review checks:**
   - Its name is the fixed word `posts`: never a DID, a handle or a display name.
   - It returns one empty page, whatever exists.
   - It makes no call to a PDS or to any other host.
   - Below it is the one not-found.
   - Its arm has no `todo!`, `unimplemented!`, `panic!` or `expect`.
   - A named test proves it: the stub answers the same bytes for alice's DID and for a DID with no user, and a child path gets the one not-found. The review records that test's `cargo test` command.
2. **Objective 2's checks.** All of them printed nothing on 2026-10-09.
   - `rg -n 'PublicRecords' backend/crates/api/src backend/crates/application/src backend/crates/composition/src backend/crates/cli/src`: the public-records port is wired nowhere.
   - `rg -n '"[^"]*/(post|blob)s?(/|")' backend/crates/api/src -g '!**/tests.rs' -g '!**/tests/**'`: there is no `/post` or `/blob` route.
   - `find frontend/web/src/routes \( -iname '*post*' -o -iname '*blob*' \)` and `rg -n 'type="file"|multipart' frontend/web/src`: the web app has no posts route and no upload form.
3. **den.proto fit:**
   - A post is a directory, and a blob is a file that a mount may target.
   - A post's pool is a set of symlinks in the server's order.
   - The segment allowlist admits atproto record keys (`A-Za-z0-9 . - _ : ~`, 1 to 512 characters, never `.` or `..`; atproto.com/specs/record-key) and blob CIDs (CIDv1, base32 with a `b` prefix; atproto.com/specs/blob).
